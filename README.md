# Monocent for Android

Download the newest Monocent app for Android:

**[monocent-mobile.apk](https://github.com/anasgrt/monocent-download/releases/latest/download/monocent-mobile.apk)**

1. Open the link on your Android phone and download the file.
2. Open the file. Android asks once to allow installing from your browser: allow it.
3. Open Monocent and sign in on **Activate Monocent** with your Monocent email and password. New? Paste your invite link too.

Updating works the same way: download the newest file and open it. Your data stays on your phone.

Each release lists the file's SHA-256 in `SHA256SUMS`.
